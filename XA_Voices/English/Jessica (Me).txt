Every morning, the small town woke up slowly. The streets were quiet, and the air was cool and fresh. People opened their windows and listened to the sounds of birds singing in the trees. Some residents enjoyed a short walk before breakfast, while others sat on their porches with a cup of coffee. The town was not famous, and it was not large, but the people who lived there appreciated its peaceful atmosphere.

In the center of the town stood an old library. The building had been there for many years and contained thousands of books. Students often visited after school to study or complete their homework. Elderly residents liked to spend time reading newspapers and magazines in the comfortable reading area. The librarian knew many visitors by name and always greeted them with a friendly smile.
